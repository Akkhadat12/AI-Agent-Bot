# The cost of a completed agent task — story structure

**Owner-approved direction, 29 September 2026.** Main question: *Will growth in completed agent work outrun the falling cost of each task?* Supporting lens: *Who controls the entry point, data, permissions, and routing of that work?* Scope: the AI value chain, with particular attention to chips, HBM, cloud, networks, data centres, and electricity. The narrative is conditional; it names no inevitable company or investment winner. The Thai [research and claim ledger](02_RESEARCH_AND_ANALYSIS.md) is the evidence authority.

## What the audience should leave with

An agent is valuable only when a task reaches a verifiable outcome. A successful task can require several model calls, tools, and human checks. Aggregate infrastructure demand rises if the volume and depth of those tasks outrun efficiency gains. Even then, the firms that receive work and control permissions can influence which models and infrastructure capture revenue. Data-centre sales and electricity figures measure broader AI activity; they are not an agent-only revenue or energy bill.

## Evidence boundaries for the narration

- Anthropic reports a correlation between autonomy and token use in its chat/Cowork sample (r = 0.68). It is an observed association in those products, not an agent-wide causal multiplier. [Anthropic Economic Index, June 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report).
- IEA describes rapidly falling energy per AI task alongside high energy for some reasoning and agentic requests. The relative pace of task growth and efficiency is unresolved. Its 485 TWh (2025) and 950 TWh (2030 central case) are for **all global data centres**, not agents. [IEA, 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary).
- NVIDIA's Q2 FY2027 Data Center revenue of $89.0B and 117% year-on-year growth cover its entire Data Center business; neither figure identifies agent demand or buyer ROI. [NVIDIA, 26 August 2026](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-second-quarter-fiscal-2027).
- Google says TPU 8i improves inference performance per dollar by 80% versus its own previous generation. This is a vendor comparison, not an independent GPU benchmark or proof of market-share transfer. [Google, 22 April 2026](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/eighth-generation-tpu-agentic-era/).
- Microsoft Agent 365 and AWS AgentCore establish that vendors are building identity, runtime, governance, and evaluation layers. Their existence does not prove durable margins. [Microsoft](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/03/09/introducing-the-first-frontier-suite-built-on-intelligence-trust/) · [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/agentcore/faqs/).
- A narrow METR 2025 developer trial found slower completion with then-current AI tools; METR says later uplift estimates are unreliable. This is a reason to measure outcomes, not a general verdict on 2026 agents. [METR, February 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/).

## Spatial and spoken arc — approximately 9 minutes 25 seconds

These are eight **settled places in one 3D journey**, not eight timed slides. The presenter may hold any place while speaking. Space and the visible scene action follow the same sequence. The path starts with a work request, follows the work into compute and physical supply, then returns to the business gate that decides where future work goes. Camera movement reveals a new causal relationship at each step; the audience never needs a menu to understand the route.

| ID / hold estimate | Argument and presenter narration | Visual reveal / route | Source anchor and guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| C0 — Cover / 0:45 | Ask what one *completed* agent task costs, rather than counting a chat prompt. Introduce a verifiable work item. | A tangible task docket occupies the foreground; a real rack is visible deeper in the scene as the physical destination. Act on the docket to enter it. | [OpenAI, outcome measurement](https://openai.com/index/managing-ai-investments-in-agentic-era/). Rack photo is an example of real infrastructure, not the machine behind this illustrative task. |
| S1 — Outcome / 1:10 | Define an agent by its ability to pursue a goal using tools with bounded permissions and checks. Separate a finished, checked output from attempted actions and token counts. Human review can be part of the cost. | Move from request to a delivered record and visible approval threshold. The completed record opens the process underneath. | [Anthropic, trustworthy agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/trustworthy-agents); [OpenAI, business value](https://openai.com/index/how-to-connect-ai-usage-to-business-value/); METR caveat above. |
| S2 — Workload / 1:15 | A single task may branch through planning, model calls, tools, retries, and checks. Workload is roughly users × tasks per user × calls per task × compute per call, adjusted for efficiency. This is a causal map, not a forecast equation. | Descend through an authored spatial work path; one visible repeated call becomes the next subject. | [OpenAI Agents API](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api/); [Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report). Do not depict every agent as using the same number of calls. |
| S3 — Counterforce / 1:15 | More tasks and deeper work push demand up; better models, routing, caching, serving, and purpose-built silicon push cost per task down. The net result is unknown. | Camera rotates around two opposing mechanical forces and settles where their combined effect is legible. Act on the convergence point to reach hardware. | [IEA efficiency discussion](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary); [Google TPU 8i vendor claim](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/eighth-generation-tpu-agentic-era/). No unsupported numeric ratio or predetermined winner. |
| S4 — Physical stack / 1:25 | Conditional incremental inference demand requires more than an accelerator: HBM, advanced packaging, CPUs, network, cooling, and racks. The supplied GB200 rack image is a recognisable physical example, not a universal architecture. | Arrive at the real rack image in a bounded frame. Authored depth layers expose memory and interconnect dependencies. Act on the memory path toward constraints. | [NVIDIA rack source](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/file/nvidia-gb200-nvl72-rack-press-graphic?action=); [IEA bottlenecks](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary). NVIDIA revenue is broad Data Center revenue, not an agent-only metric. |
| S5 — Capacity / 1:25 | Physical supply is constrained by HBM and by power equipment, grid connection, cooling, and capital. The IEA global data-centre electricity central case rises from 485 TWh in 2025 to 950 TWh in 2030, but that does not estimate the agent share. | Move along a real-feeling rack-to-grid corridor; pause at an exact, clearly labelled two-point chart. Act on the grid connection to move back up to routing decisions. | [IEA, 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary). Chart data in [references/iea-data-centre-electricity-2025-2030.csv](references/iea-data-centre-electricity-2025-2030.csv). |
| S6 — Control point / 1:25 | The app or enterprise system that holds the task, context, data, and rights can select a platform, model, and compute path. A Google TPU 8i board is a real alternate silicon example; neither it nor a GPU is guaranteed to capture every task. Governance and reliability affect routing. | Camera rises from physical capacity to an authored permission gate, with the real TPU board integrated as one route. Act on the approved task path to see the conditional answer. | [Google TPU 8i](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/eighth-generation-tpu-agentic-era/); [Microsoft Agent 365](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/03/09/introducing-the-first-frontier-suite-built-on-intelligence-trust/); [AWS AgentCore](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/agentcore/faqs/). Product launches are not market-share evidence. |
| E0 — Conclusion / 0:45 | Ask three monitorable questions: Are verified completed tasks growing? Is full cost per task falling faster or slower? Who controls permission and routing? The answer determines how strongly agent demand reaches supply and who may capture value. | Settle on the same delivered record from S1, now connected to a conditional cost and capacity path. Let the presenter hold or reset to cover. | No forecast, company ranking, stock call, or claim that all data-centre demand is agent-driven. |

**Estimated total: 9:25.** The exact presentation can run 8–12 minutes because each scene is a pauseable state. The web page should carry only sparse English labels; the explanation above belongs in narration and supporting documents.
