Test the **public Vercel production URL** for the final build on [`agent-bot-ai-supply-chain-2026`](https://github.com/Akkhadat12/AI-Agent-Bot/tree/agent-bot-ai-supply-chain-2026), not the old site or a preview. Evidence must show the final published version; never fabricate a screenshot. A claim can fail through the **visual implication** even if its sentence is technically true.

## Twelve mandatory review perspectives

1. **Evidence and factual integrity.** Reopen primary sources at publication time. Audit every visible number, year, unit, claim, label, chart, source image, and visual implication. Distinguish observation, vendor claim, announced plan, scenario, and our inference. The IEA chart is all data centres; Anthropic's r=0.68 is product-specific association; NVIDIA's $89.0B is total Data Center revenue; Google's 80% is vendor comparison to its prior TPU. Verify no claim extends beyond these scopes.
2. **Story and thesis integrity.** Follow C0→E0 without the brief. Confirm the outcome→workload→efficiency counterforce→physical stack→capacity→routing→conditional answer logic. Fail a missing beat, repeated decorative beat, or a conclusive winner that replaces the owner-approved question.
3. **Visual communication and composition.** Temporarily hide titles and ask whether each scene's objects and spatial relationship still communicate its main idea. Inspect hierarchy, contrast, depth, perspective change, and scene differentiation. Fail flat slide stacks, generic AI node networks, repeated cards, empty recolored stages, or generic decorative effects.
4. **Visual polish and spatial composition.** At each primary settled frame, perform a brief **first-glance focal test** without the brief. The intended object or relationship should attract attention first. Check margins, alignment, scale, negative space, z-order, connector endpoints, label proximity, accidental tangencies, intentional crop, and balanced visual weight. Flag clutter, drifting labels, weak focus, near-edge objects, and oversized/undersized subjects unless the topic-specific rationale explains them.
5. **Real-asset and image integrity.** On the published site, inspect the rack in C0/S4 and TPU board in S6 against files in `references/` and original source links in [04](04_BUILD_WEB.md). Verify actual rendering, source notes, proportions, resolution, safe-area crop, edge/mask quality, identity, and no collision. Fail broken paths, fake marks, invented likeness, misleading edits, or cropped-away identifying features.
6. **Presenter and recording usability.** Use the website as a spoken visual aid at 16:9. All eight states must settle and remain readable for arbitrary duration. No scene timer, distracting perpetual animation, fixed navigation strip, dots, page numbers, arrows, keyboard legend, or fixed advance button in capture. Cover must have a visible entry point; English copy should remain minimal and legible.
7. **Interaction and motion quality.** Test every target in the Interaction Map by mouse and every Space step separately. Test hover/focus, R from each state, fast repeated keys/clicks, double activation, interrupted transitions, and settled continuity. Motion must explain relationship then stop. Fail stuck states, accidental skipped scenes, jank, flashes, ambiguous targets, or an experience with no intentional motion.
8. **Desktop usability, accessibility, and reduced motion.** Test 1920×1080 and 1280×720 plus practical desktop resizing. Check overflow, wrap, image fit, focus visibility, keyboard operation, meaningful accessible names, contrast, and `prefers-reduced-motion` preserving content/route while reducing travel. Phone layout, touch testing, and phone screenshots are not required.
9. **Technical reliability and performance.** Confirm a clean production build, successful first load, all assets loaded, no uncaught errors or repeated material console failures, stable animation and refresh, resize resilience, no obvious event-listener/memory growth after repeated route cycles, and recovery from fast input. Build success alone is insufficient if it visibly breaks.
10. **Deployment and public access.** Open the exact production URL in a clean real browser without builder login, refresh, reopen directly, and verify it serves the final commit/assets. A private session, stale production build, old reported site, or preview URL fails.
11. **Adversarial and regression review.** Attempt to falsify completeness: cycle C0→E0 more than once, reset from each state, resize during a transition, hide titles, reload, trigger repeated inputs, and revisit every changed scene after fixes. Recheck transitions and evidence after any asset or palette change.
12. **Owner-facing scene rationale.** Open the current Thai `06_SCENE_RATIONALE` Google Doc or one `06_SCENE_RATIONALE.docx` in the linked Drive folder. It must show actual final published screenshots of cover, each content scene, and closing; explain each scene's narrative purpose, visual choice, evidence/caveat, and any deviation from plan; give the exact public URL. Fail missing, stale, unreadable, or materially inconsistent rationale.

## Reporting and handoff protocol

Create `07_WEB_QA_REPORT.md` on the **same branch**. For each scene and relevant perspective, record expected versus observed, `PASS` / `FAIL` / `PARTIAL`, viewport/state, actual screenshot or precise browser observation, source reference, and date. Give each finding a stable ID (`QA-001`, etc.), severity, owner, requested correction, affected scenes, and retest result. Link the final production URL and exact deployed commit. Use actual evidence, not reconstructed descriptions presented as screenshots.

If any acceptance check fails, set [WORKFLOW_STATUS.md](WORKFLOW_STATUS.md) to `QA_FAIL` with finding IDs and `Next: Build`. The builder fixes on the same branch, updates `BUILD_NOTES.md`, production deploy, rationale/screenshots if affected, and sets `READY_FOR_QA` with new URL/commit; QA retests fixes **and regressions**. Only mark `QA_PASS` when required checks pass and the report includes final published screenshots, test metadata, source audit, and scene rationale verification. Record a true blocker explicitly; do not silently waive one.
