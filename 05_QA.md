## Evidence and factual integrity

**Whole site, every claim and visual:** On the final publication date, reopen the primary links in `02_` and log the date checked. Classify each displayed element as announcement, vendor-reported result, independent causal study, observational usage, analysis or scenario. Compare exact wording, units, periods and populations with sources. **FAIL** a claim that Grok Bot and OpenAI Agents API are the same product; a claim that an announced beta proves business ROI; a quantified sector ranking or profit figure without data; or an unqualified claim that agents replace whole occupations.

**S4:** If “99%” appears, it must be explicitly SpaceXAI's own refund-resolution claim for its own support workflow; “14%” is issues per hour for 5,179 human support agents using an AI assistant in an NBER study. Their denominators, products and designs differ. **FAIL** a shared bar chart or a claim that NBER verified Grok Bot. Prefer these figures in narration rather than on screen. Evidence: source text plus screenshot of any displayed values/labels.

**S5:** Anthropic's roughly 400,000 sessions are observed Claude Code usage from Oct 2025–Apr 2026. METR's 19% slower estimate is from experienced open-source developers on early-2025 tasks; its later study does not give a reliable precise 2026 uplift. **FAIL** if usage becomes productivity, 19% becomes a universal current estimate, or the time/sample context is hidden when the number is displayed. Evidence: source and screenshot.

**S6:** Choco's “over 8.8 million annual orders” and “up to 50%” manual entry reduction are a vendor/customer case, not industry averages. OpenAI changed its checkout strategy; this is friction, not proof of failure of all commerce agents. **FAIL** an invented Choco UI/product SKU, a misleading average, or a shopping system shown as already autonomously controlling payment and returns. Evidence: source and screen.

**S3/S7/Closing:** The value equation and task gates are analytic concepts, never measured scales or proven thresholds. **FAIL** proportional areas, axes, scores or badges implying quantitative ROI, safety or success without a real dataset. Verify that the conditional conclusion survives even with titles hidden.

## Story and visual communication

Check the finished sequence against Cover → S1 → S2 → S3 → S4 → S5 → S6 → S7 → Closing. Each settled screen must do a different job: puzzle, definition, two product routes, economics, support example, coding counterpoint, structured orders and checkout friction, conditions, conditional conclusion. In the browser and screenshots, temporarily hide each title and judge whether the subjects/relationships still convey that scene's core point. Run a **first-glance focal test** at the recorded 16:9 view before reading the brief: note what draws attention first; it should be the intended work relationship, not decoration, empty space or a small incidental label. **FAIL** missing counterevidence, interchangeable cards, repeated composition without narrative reason, text-only/recolored empty stages, generic glowing networks, generic AI image backgrounds, fake interfaces, or scene repetition that removes the argument. Intentional cover/closing work-item repetition must make the final condition clear.

For every screen inspect alignment, size, negative space, margins, hierarchy, label proximity, connector endpoints, image fit, z-order, accidental tangencies, crop quality, and visual weight. Evaluate the actual stage, typography and accent colors against the visual goals and the builder's browser comparison of two materially different color directions. Record the chosen hex values in `07_WEB_QA_REPORT.md`. A previous project's palette is neither automatic pass nor fail. **FAIL** a busy or weak-contrast background, a subject too small to be focal, oversized decoration, or imagery that overwhelms spoken narration. Still scenes pass; only motion that clarifies state adds value.

## Scene composition and clipping acceptance

Inspect the **actual published page** in a real browser at a desktop 16:9 recording viewport and at least one narrow phone-width viewport. Capture the settled cover and **each** settled scene at 16:9, a narrow screenshot for each distinct layout, and representative transition/hover/focus states that can clip content. Inspect screenshots at normal viewing scale and after resize/navigation, not only CSS dimensions. For each row record expected subject, observed screenshot/browser evidence, PASS/FAIL/PARTIAL and any fix/retest. No page-level horizontal overflow. An intentional crop passes only if its purpose is documented and identity/evidence remains visible at both sizes. Any accidental clipping, unreadable image, lost detail, off-screen text or click target, stretched proportion, cutout artifact, collision, overlap or awkward edge placement is **FAIL** even if the build passes; the builder must fix the composition/asset and recapture, then QA rechecks the final deployed page.

## Presenter, interaction and motion coverage

The production URL must land directly on the **stable dedicated cover**, which has a subject-relevant focal composition beyond a title and a visible clickable work item entering S1. Every settled scene must remain readable for as long as the owner speaks, with no timed advance or motion pressure. Verify text is English-only, minimal (target ≤12 visible authored words per scene excluding indispensable labels/data), readable at the actual capture scale, and free of page numbers, arrows, carousel dots, rails and source citations on the stage. Count visible words; document justified exceptions. **FAIL** if the recording crop/browser chrome hides important subjects, words or targets, or if a source screenshot overwhelms the stage.

Test the actual interaction map (or the builder's documented final equivalent): Cover work item → S1; S1 record → S2; S2 shared workplace tool → S3; S3 work item → S4; S4 escalation flag → S5; S5 test result → S6; S6 validated order → S7; S7 passing task → Closing; Closing work item → Cover. From Cover, S4 and Closing, test Space and R: Space advances one from Cover or S4 and **holds** on Closing; R returns to Cover from all three. From every scene, click the visible target and observe correct destination/feedback. Repeat clicks and Space during a transition and after it settles; **FAIL** double advance, skip, stuck state, invisible click region with no affordance, focus loss, or a shortcut firing while typing into an input. Inspect representative transition frames for clipping, flashes and collisions.

Test `prefers-reduced-motion`: information and interactions remain available with reduced or no movement. Verify movement settles rather than floating/pulsing indefinitely. Test keyboard focus visibility and accessible names for the cover entry and each meaningful target; touch targets must work on narrow view. **FAIL** motion jank, ambiguous state, forced timing, perpetual decorative motion, missing focus, inaccessible meaningful control, or navigation that works only with one input method.

## Responsive, technical and deployment review

On desktop and phone: load fresh, resize while on S2/S4/S7, navigate back and forth, reload on cover and a middle scene, and attempt rapid repeated input. Check wrapping, fit, aspect ratios, no horizontal overflow, no off-screen controls and no unreadable text. Inspect console/runtime and network for missing local assets, uncaught errors, repeated warnings affecting usability, event-listener buildup or visible freezes. A clean build without a stable usable page is **FAIL**.

Open the **actual final production URL** in a fresh browser context without the builder's login, load and reopen it, verify all screens and assets and ensure it is the same URL returned to the owner. A preview, private/authenticated page, stale deployment or localhost is **FAIL**. In the recording check use a practical 16:9 full-viewport capture scale and record whether browser chrome, scaling, letterboxing or capture crop changes the safe area. Recheck the affected scenes after every material fix and ensure no regression in neighboring screens.
